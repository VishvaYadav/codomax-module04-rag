# Nimbus Labs Employee Handbook (Fictional Sample Document)

> This is a made-up company handbook created to demonstrate Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
> None of these policies belong to a real organization. Because the content is fictional, a language
> model cannot know it from training data. It can only answer correctly by retrieving it.

## 1. About Nimbus Labs

Nimbus Labs is a fictional robotics software company founded in 2019 by Meera Iyer and Daniel Okafor.
The headquarters is in Pune, India, with a smaller engineering office in Lisbon, Portugal.
The company builds navigation software for warehouse robots. Its flagship product is called
**PathPilot**, and the second product, **DockSense**, helps robots find charging stations automatically.
Nimbus Labs has 140 employees as of 2026.

## 2. Working Hours and Remote Work

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees may
plan their own schedule. Nimbus Labs follows a hybrid model: employees work from the office on
**Tuesdays and Thursdays**, and may work remotely on the other weekdays. Fully remote work requires
written approval from a department head and is reviewed every six months.

## 3. Leave Policy

Every full-time employee receives **24 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year. Up to 8 unused
days can be carried forward to the next year; any additional unused days expire on 31 March.
Employees also receive 10 days of paid sick leave. Sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days requires
a medical certificate. Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the
secondary caregiver. Leave requests are submitted in the internal HR portal called **Nest** at least
two weeks in advance, except for sick leave.

## 4. Probation and Performance Reviews

New employees have a **probation period of 6 months**. During probation, the notice period is 15 days;
after confirmation it becomes 60 days. Performance reviews happen twice a year, in **April and October**.
Each review uses a 5-point scale, and ratings of 4 or 5 make an employee eligible for the annual
performance bonus, which is paid in June.

## 5. Learning and Development

Each employee has a **learning budget of ₹40,000 per year** for courses, certifications, books and
conference tickets. Unused budget does not carry over. Employees can also take 5 paid "learning days"
per year to study or attend workshops. Requests above ₹15,000 for a single item need manager approval.
Every Friday from 16:00 to 17:00, the company hosts an internal knowledge-sharing session called
**Cloud Talks**.

## 6. Equipment and IT

New employees receive a laptop on their first day. Engineers may choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro
and a Linux laptop with 32 GB RAM. Laptops are replaced every **3 years**, or earlier if they fail.
Employees receive a one-time home-office allowance of ₹25,000 to buy a chair, monitor or desk.
IT support is available through the **#it-help** Slack channel from 09:00 to 19:00 IST.

## 7. Information Security

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts. Passwords must be at least
14 characters long and stored only in the company password manager, **Vaultwarden**.
Any suspected security incident, such as a lost laptop or a phishing email that was clicked,
must be reported to **security@nimbuslabs.example** within **2 hours** of discovery.
Customer robot data must never be copied to personal devices or uploaded to public AI tools.

## 8. Travel and Expenses

Business travel must be booked through the travel desk at least 7 days in advance when possible.
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.
The daily meal allowance during business travel is **₹2,500 in India** and **€60 abroad**.
Expense claims must be submitted with receipts within **30 days** of the expense date, using the
Nest portal. Claims submitted after 30 days need approval from the finance team.

## 9. On-Call and Support

Engineers on the PathPilot team join an on-call rotation of one week at a time, roughly once every
six weeks. On-call engineers receive an allowance of ₹6,000 per on-call week. The target response time
for critical incidents (severity 1) is **15 minutes**, and for severity 2 incidents it is 2 hours.

## 10. Code of Conduct

Nimbus Labs expects respectful, inclusive behaviour from everyone. Harassment of any kind is not
tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR, or anonymously through the **Ethics Line**.
Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.
